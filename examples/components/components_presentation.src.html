Problem – Insight – Method – Result

Problem:
Scaling CNNs by tuning depth, width, or resolution — but always one at a time, which is suboptimal
Insight:
Depth, width, and resolution are interdependent — scaling them together in a fixed ratio results in better accuracy & efficiency
Method:
A compound scaling rule (ϕ) to jointly scale depth, width, and resolution together
Result:
EfficientNet — a family of models (B0–B7) that outperform prior networks at a fraction of the parameters

Compound scaling rule – constraints

Subject to the constraint:
α · β2 · γ2 ≈ 2      with   α ≥ 1,  β ≥ 1,  γ ≥ 1
FLOPS scale differently per dimension:
  • Doubling depth → FLOPS ×2  (linear)
  • Doubling width → FLOPS ×4  (quadratic)
  • Doubling resolution → FLOPS ×4  (quadratic, pixels scale in 2D)
Each ϕ step → total FLOPS × 2ϕ
