4. How panels work

Each sidebar card belongs to one panel (same data-panel id). Clicking a card opens or closes its panel. At most 3 panels are open; a 4th closes the one opened longest ago. Open panels always appear in sidebar order.

Deck slides open panels with data-params="open=scaling,grid".
